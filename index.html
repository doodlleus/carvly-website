How it works

From an idea to a carving, all on your phone

New to CNC? A step-by-step tutorial makes your first sign with you, from the design to the machine.

  1. 1Design it

    Type your words, draw with your finger, or start from a ready-made sign, chopping board or coasters. A few questions set up the whole project.

  2. 2See it in 3D

    Carvly plans the cuts for your bit and your wood, then shows the carved board in 3D. Every job is checked against the board's edges and your machine's reach.

  3. 3Carve it

    Send it to your machine over WiFi or a USB cable, set the starting point, and follow the carving live, with pause and stop always in reach.

Everything in one app

Made for your phone, not squeezed onto it

  • New to CNC? Start here

    • A tutorial that makes your first sign with you.
    • Makers for signs, chopping boards, coasters and keyring tags.
    • Plain words, not jargon. Feeds and speeds are suggested for your bit and your wood.

  • Design with your fingers

    • Shapes, lines, freehand drawing, text, borders and layers, with undo.
    • Import SVG and DXF files, or turn a picture or photo into a carving.
    • Photograph a drawing on paper, even a child's, and carve it.

  • Every kind of cut

    • True V-carving with crisp corners.
    • Pockets, cut-outs with holding tabs, holes, reliefs and inlays.
    • Jobs with more than one bit pause for a guided bit change.

  • See it before you cut it

    • A realistic picture of the carved board, and a 3D view to turn.
    • Every job is checked: its depth, the board's edges and the machine's reach.
    • A time estimate for each bit.

  • Run the machine from your phone

    • Home, jog and set the starting point with paper or a touch plate.
    • Trace the outline to check the clamps before you carve.
    • Notifications when a bit needs changing or the job is done.

  • Phones, tablets and folds

    • Layouts for small phones, tablets and folding phones, open or closed.
    • Light and dark themes, and large text.

Your machine

Connects by WiFi or USB cable

  • SainSmart Genmitsu with the WiFi module

    The machines Carvly is built for first. Your phone talks to the module over WiFi.

  • GRBL, grblHAL and FluidNC

    Other machines connect by USB cable or WiFi too.

  • Anything else

    Export the G-code and load it the machine's own way.
